Offline identification catalog generated for KUBA Nearby Scanner.

MAC vendor assignments:
IEEE Registration Authority public OUI registry
https://standards-oui.ieee.org/oui/oui.csv

Bluetooth company identifiers, service UUIDs and appearance values:
Bluetooth SIG public Assigned Numbers repository
https://bitbucket.org/bluetooth-SIG/public/src/main/assigned_numbers/

The registries identify assigned organizations and protocol values. A vendor match
does not prove the exact product model or device owner. Random/private MAC addresses
cannot be resolved through an IEEE OUI.
